Services

Immigration.
Social services.
Know your rights.

We offer free, confidential legal and social services in English and Arabic.

Immigration

Get legal help with family petitions, asylum, and representation if you or a family member is detained by ICE or has a court date.

Social services

Apply for public benefits such as food stamps (CalFresh), health insurance (Medi‑Cal), and cash assistance (CalWORKs).

Know your rights

Learn what to do if immigration agents come to your home, work, or community. Free trainings for families, businesses, and community groups.

Eligibility

Is this for you?

We serve working-class Arab, Muslim, North African, and Southwest Asian residents of the Bay.

Requirements

  • ✺You or someone in your household is a resident of the Greater SF Bay Area.
  • ✺Your household income falls within the low-to-moderate income range.
  • ✺You or someone in your household is Arab, Muslim, North African, or Southwest Asian.

Not sure? Call anyway. If we can’t help, we’ll connect you with someone who can.

Process

What to expect

A three-step intake.

How it works

  1. ➊Call us at (415) 861-7444.
  2. ➋We’ll follow up to schedule a consultation and assess whether we’re the right team for your case.
  3. ➌If we take on your case, we’ll gather your documents and get to work. If we can’t take on your case, we’ll refer you to trusted organizations and attorneys.

Privacy

What you share with us stays strictly confidential. We never share your information with immigration authorities or anyone else, without your consent.

About

Who we are

Lawyers and advocates reuniting families and defending people held by ICE since 2007.

Mission

Arab Community Services protects Arabs and Muslims through free immigration representation, social services, and know-your-rights training.

Team

Our team includes two immigration attorneys, one DOJ-accredited representative, three legal assistants, and one social services coordinator.

History

We began in 2007 as Arab American Legal Services (AALS), a program of the Arab Resource and Organizing Center (AROC). In 2026, we became ACS, our own organization.

AROC

Our sister organization, AROC, organizes working-class Arabs and Muslims in the Bay Area against racism, forced migration, and militarism.
